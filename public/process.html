How It Works

Our Process

No surprises, no vague scopes. Here's exactly how a project runs from your first call to the final walkthrough.

1

Call or Request an Estimate

Call (360) 338-2000 or send your project details and a few photos through the estimate form — you'll hear back the same day.

2

On-Site Walkthrough & Quote

We look at the actual scope in person, talk through color and product options, and provide a clear, itemized quote — no vague allowances or hidden add-ons.

3

Schedule the Work

Once you approve the quote, we lock in a start date and walk you through what to expect day-to-day.

4

Prep

Pressure washing, scraping, sanding, caulking, rotted wood replacement, priming bare or worn areas, and masking — the unglamorous work that determines how long the finish actually lasts.

Window masking and surface prep before spraying
5

Paint or Stain

Premium Sherwin-Williams products applied by spray and back-roll for full, even coverage, with multiple coats where the spec calls for it, and daily site cleanup — no mess left sitting overnight.

Application in progress
6

Final Walkthrough

We walk the finished job with you before calling it done, and handle any touch-ups on the spot. We hold every job to the Painting Contractors Association (PCA) standard for a properly painted surface: uniform in color, texture, hiding, and sheen, with no drips, holidays, or overspray visible without magnification from 39 inches under normal lighting.

Materials We Use

Product selected for what the surface actually needs — not a single house-brand product used everywhere. Specs below are from Sherwin-Williams' published product data sheets.

Duration Exterior Acrylic

Premium siding & trim repaints

Thick-mil, high-build acrylic coating built for maximum exterior durability.

Coverage: 250–300 sq ft/gal · Recoat: 4 hrs (45°F+), 24–48 hrs (35–45°F)

SuperPaint Exterior

General exterior repaints

Self-priming vinyl acrylic with strong hide and adhesion for everyday exterior work.

Coverage: 350–400 sq ft/gal · Touch dry: 1 hr · Recoat: 4 hrs (77°F/50% RH)

Latitude Exterior Acrylic

Cool & shoulder-season painting

ClimateFlex technology applies down to 35°F where standard exterior latex quits around 50°F — keeps PNW project timelines moving through fall and early spring.

Application range: 35–120°F · Rain-ready in as little as 30 min at 35°F

Emerald Urethane Trim Enamel

Trim, doors & cabinets

Waterbased urethane-modified alkyd — the look, flow, and durability of an oil enamel without the oil-based cleanup or yellowing.

Coverage: 350–400 sq ft/gal · Recoat: 4 hrs · Full cure: 7–30 days

WoodScapes Stain

Log homes, cedar siding & shakes

Film-forming, self-priming, mildew-resistant pigmented stain for siding, shakes, fascia, soffits, trim, fences, and log-home walls.

Coverage: 200–400 sq ft/gal (varies with wood porosity) · Recoat: 24 hrs

SuperDeck Stain

Decks & fences

Solid, semi-solid, and semi-transparent formulations applied by sprayer, roller, or brush, always with the grain.

Coverage: 200–400 sq ft/gal · Dry: 4+ hrs between coats
