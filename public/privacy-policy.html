Shoreline Pro Services LLC ("we," "us," "our") operates shorelineproservices.com. This page explains what information we collect from visitors and how it's used.

Information We Collect

When you submit the estimate request form, we collect the information you provide: name, phone number, project type, any project details you include, and any photos or videos you choose to attach. We do not require an email address, and we do not collect payment information through this site.

How We Use It

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties for marketing purposes.

This site may use Google Ads and Google Analytics to measure ad performance and site usage. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to understand how visitors interact with the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and opt out of personalized Google advertising at adssettings.google.com.

Data Retention

We retain estimate requests for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records, typically no longer than a few years.
