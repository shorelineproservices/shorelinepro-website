# Shoreline Pro Services Website Strategy Brief

## Business positioning
- Business: Shoreline Pro Services LLC
- Service: residential painting contractor
- Geography: Clark County, Washington, with emphasis on Vancouver, WA
- Value proposition: reliable, premium-quality residential painting with clear communication, fair pricing, and clean workmanship
- Business model: lead generation through local search, reviews, referrals, and neighborhood visibility

## Core strategic recommendation
The most reliable and cost-effective stack for this business is:
- GitHub as the project source of truth
- Netlify for deployment and hosting
- Cloudflare only for DNS/proxy and domain management
- WordPress and Railway not used as primary production hosts for the core website

This matches the goal of reducing operational noise, consolidating tools, and improving performance without unnecessary recurring platform overhead.

## Target audience
- Homeowners planning interior or exterior painting
- Owners wanting a professional, responsive contractor for residential work
- People searching for term-specific services in Clark County and nearby communities
- Customers responding to discovery, review, and referral signals

## Brand priorities
- Trust and professionalism
- Local expertise
- Clear estimates and communication
- Premium finish quality with honest pricing
- Strong before/after proof and review validation

## Content priorities
- Real Google reviews
- Real job photography
- Service-specific pages for interior, exterior, cabinet refinishing, deck/fence staining
- Local service pages for Vancouver, Camas, Battle Ground, Washougal, Ridgefield
- FAQ content for homeowner decision-making
- Strong calls to action and phone-first conversion

## Conversion goals
- Get calls from local search and ads
- Capture quote requests through simple, non-noisy lead forms
- Present premium trust markers without fake claims or invented review data
- Drive repeat work and referrals from past customers

## Guardrails
- Use real reviews and real photos only.
- Keep the site performance-focused and mobile-first.
- Prioritize local SEO and conversion quality over cluttered design.
- Avoid fake social proof, generic stock imagery, or inflated claims.
