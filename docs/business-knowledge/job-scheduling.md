# Job Scheduling

## Scheduling goals
- Maximize crew productivity while minimizing travel and idle time.
- Protect enough buffer time for prep, cleanup, and delays.
- Prioritize quality over overbooking.

## Standard scheduling fields
- Customer name
- Property address
- Job type
- Crew assigned
- Start date and time
- Scope summary
- Material and equipment needed
- Estimated duration
- Weather or access considerations

## Recommended workflow
1. Confirm customer approval and project scope.
2. Check crew availability and material readiness.
3. Schedule a realistic start date with contingency time.
4. Send reminders before the job.
5. Review the day before to verify supply list and access details.

## Scheduling rules
- Reserve buffer time for exterior jobs affected by weather.
- Keep enough room for patching and detail work.
- Avoid stacking too many jobs in one week if prep and cleanup are heavy.
- Track job completion status and follow-up tasks.

## Job closeout checklist
- Final walkthrough with customer
- Photo documentation
- Warranty or maintenance reminder
- Invoice trigger
- Referral or review request
