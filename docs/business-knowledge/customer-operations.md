# Customer Operations

## Business context
- Business: Shoreline Pro Services LLC
- Service area: Clark County, Washington
- Primary customer type: residential homeowners
- Primary services: interior painting, exterior painting, cabinets, repairs, custom finishing

## Intake workflow
1. Capture name, phone, email, property address, and service need.
2. Confirm whether the customer is seeking an estimate, repaint, cabinet work, exterior refresh, or specialty project.
3. Record urgency, timeline, budget range, and any prior contractor history.
4. Save references, previous paint colors, and preferred communication method.
5. Schedule estimate visit or virtual consult.

## Estimate process
- Collect photos of the project area when possible.
- Confirm job scope before quoting: surfaces, prep work, trim, doors, windows, ceilings, repairs, stain blocking, and cleanup expectations.
- Clarify whether the quote includes labor, materials, masking, caulking, patching, primer, and warranty.
- Provide written estimate with assumptions and exclusions.

## Communication standards
- Respond quickly and professionally without overpromising.
- Confirm schedule changes by text or email.
- Use clear, plain-language explanations for scope and pricing.
- Review any change orders before additional work begins.

## Follow-up best practices
- After estimate, send recap summary.
- Track whether customer accepted, declined, or requested revisions.
- Follow up within 48–72 hours on warm leads.
- Capture notes on customer preferences, concerns, and decision triggers.
