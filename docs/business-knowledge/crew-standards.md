# Crew Standards and Operations

## Crew expectations
- Show up on time and professionally dressed
- Maintain job-site cleanliness and safety
- Communicate clearly with customer and project lead
- Protect surfaces, floors, landscaping, and entry areas
- Follow approved scope and quality expectations

## Quality standards
- Surface prep is as important as the finish coat
- Consistent cuts, edges, and coverage matter more than speed
- Patch and caulk details should be checked before final walk-through
- Protect and clean all work areas before leaving the site

## Daily field checklist
- Verify tools, materials, and PPE
- Confirm schedule and customer access
- Check weather and site conditions
- Document progress with photos
- Review remaining tasks and next-day priorities

## Safety and professionalism
- Use safe ladder practices and proper ventilation
- Handle paint and chemicals according to label instructions
- Secure jobsites and avoid working in unsafe conditions
- Keep customer communication respectful and concise

## Crew communication
- One point of contact for each project
- Daily updates on progress, delays, or change orders
- Clear handoff between estimate, scheduling, and completion
