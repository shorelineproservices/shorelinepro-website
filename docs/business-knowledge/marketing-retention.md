# Marketing and Retention

## Core message
Shoreline Pro Services LLC should position itself as a reliable, detail-focused residential painting partner for Clark County homeowners who want clean execution, clear communication, and honest pricing.

## Marketing themes
- Clean, professional residential painting
- Honest estimates and clear scope
- Quality prep before finish
- Respectful, tidy job-site experience
- Exterior refresh and interior transformation

## Referral strategy
- Ask for referrals after successful completion
- Offer a simple referral thank-you or discount for future work
- Keep a customer follow-up cadence for repaint maintenance and touch-ups

## Retention and repeat work
- Track repaint timing and maintenance recommendations
- Schedule periodic check-ins with past customers
- Capture before/after project photos for portfolio and social proof
- Encourage reviews from happy clients

## Local positioning
- Emphasize Clark County, Vancouver, and nearby residential neighborhoods
- Lead with reliability, professionalism, and clean workmanship
- Use local references and project examples where possible
