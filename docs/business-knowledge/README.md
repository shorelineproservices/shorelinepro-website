Use this folder for:

- customer intake workflows
- job estimate SOPs
- scheduling and crew planning
- pricing and labor assumptions
- communication templates
- service quality standards
- marketing and retention ideas
- operational checklists

## Core business context

- Business name: Shoreline Pro Services LLC
- Market: Residential painting contractor
- Service area: Clark County, Washington
- Default geography: Vancouver, WA / surrounding Clark County areas
- Business model: residential repainting, interior/exterior, cabinet, and custom work

## Operating priorities

1. Fast customer response and quote turnaround
2. Clean job communication and job-site professionalism
3. Consistent estimating and scope clarity
4. Reliable scheduling and crew coordination

6. Repeat business and referrals
